# 1. Executive Summary

The objective is not to build another football club website.

The objective is to build the benchmark digital platform for grassroots football in Ireland and Europe.

The platform must:

- Welcome families.
- Recruit players.
- Support coaches.
- Reduce volunteer administration.
- Attract sponsors.
- Celebrate the community.
- Scale for the next ten years.

Every recommendation must improve at least one of:

- Visitor experience
- Community engagement
- Volunteer efficiency
- Technical quality
- Accessibility
- Security
- Performance
- Long-term maintainability

---

# 2. Vision & Mission

The website is the digital front door of River Valley Rangers AFC.

It should feel like a premium sports brand while remaining warm, inclusive and community-focused.

Visitors should immediately think:

- This is well organised.
- This is welcoming.
- This is active.
- This is somewhere I want to belong.

# 9. UX Standards

Assume over 80% of visitors are on mobile.

Design for a parent standing pitch-side.

Navigation must expose:

- Join
- Fixtures
- Results
- Contact
- Campaigns
- Teams

No page should require zooming.

# 27. Master AI Review Prompt

Act as a multidisciplinary digital consultancy including specialists in product design, UX, UI, branding, photography, marketing, SEO, accessibility, Next.js, Vercel, OWASP security, analytics, AI automation and grassroots football operations.

Phase 1:
Perform a complete evidence-based audit of the River Valley Rangers website. Identify strengths, weaknesses, risks and opportunities. Prioritise recommendations by impact and effort.

Phase 2:
Design the next evolution of the platform without losing the club's identity. Build around mobile-first design, authentic photography, campaign management, automation, accessibility, security, performance and long-term maintainability.

Challenge assumptions. Recommend only features that improve visitor experience, reduce volunteer effort, strengthen the community or achieve multiple objectives simultaneously.

Deliver:
- Executive summary
- Prioritised recommendations
- Security report
- Vercel report
- UX/UI report
- Marketing report
- SEO report
- Accessibility report
- Technical roadmap
- 30/90/365 day plan
- 3-year product vision.
